About Prabhas

From a promising debut to becoming a global icon — the extraordinary journey of the Rebel Star.

Born Oct 23, 1979 · Hyderabad

The Pan-Indian Superstar

Uppalapati Venkata Suryanarayana Prabhas Raju — known simply as Prabhas — is one of Indian cinema's most bankable superstars, working predominantly in Telugu cinema and now as a true pan-India force.

He is the first Indian actor to have a wax statue at Madame Tussauds in Los Angeles. His historic dedication of five full years exclusively to the Baahubali franchise — turning down all other projects — stands as a testament to his unparalleled commitment to his craft.

His portrayal of Amarendra and Mahendra Baahubali under S.S. Rajamouli's direction became a cultural phenomenon. Since then, he has continued expanding his universe with Saaho, Radhe Shyam, Adipurush, Salaar, and the upcoming Spirit with Sandeep Reddy Vanga.
