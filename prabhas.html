About Prabhas

From a promising debut to becoming a global icon, Prabhas' journey is nothing short of extraordinary.

The Pan-Indian Superstar

Uppalapati Venkata Suryanarayana Prabhas Raju, known mononymously as Prabhas, is an Indian actor who works predominantly in Telugu cinema. Born on October 23, 1979, he is the first Indian actor to have a wax statue at Madame Tussaud's wax museum in Los Angeles.

Prabhas made his acting debut with the 2002 drama film "Eeswar". He gained recognition with his role in "Varsham" (2004) and later achieved stardom with blockbuster hits like "Chatrapathi" (2005), "Mirchi" (2013), and the "Baahubali" series (2015-2017).

His portrayal of Amarendra Baahubali and Mahendra Baahubali in S.S. Rajamouli's epic fantasy film "Baahubali: The Beginning" (2015) and its sequel "Baahubali 2: The Conclusion" (2017) earned him nationwide fame and several awards. The films broke numerous box office records, making Prabhas a pan-Indian star.

Career Timeline

Debut Film

Baahubali: The Beginning

Portrayed the dual roles of Amarendra Baahubali and Mahendra Baahubali, achieving nationwide fame.

2017

Baahubali 2: The Conclusion

The sequel broke all box office records, becoming the highest-grossing Indian film at that time.

2019

Saaho

Starred in the action thriller "Saaho", which was released in multiple languages.

2022

Radhe Shyam

Played the lead role in the romantic drama "Radhe Shyam" set in Europe during the 1970s.

2023

Salaar

Starred in the action thriller "Salaar: Part 1 - Ceasefire" directed by Prashanth Neel.

Did You Know?

Record Breaker

Prabhas is the first South Indian actor to receive a wax statue at Madame Tussaud's in Los Angeles.

Dedicated to Craft

He dedicated 5 years of his career exclusively to the Baahubali franchise, refusing other projects during that time.

Physical Transformation

For Salaar, Prabhis gained 20kg of muscle mass, undergoing intense physical training for over a year.
